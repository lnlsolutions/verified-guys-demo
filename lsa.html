How a lead moves

Every call, message and booking request becomes a CRM record with the source attached.
LSA lead→ Call or message→ CRM record→ Follow-up queue→ Estimate booked
  • Missed calls trigger an automatic text back within a minute, which is where most roofing leads are lost
  • Lead source is stamped on the record, so cost per booked estimate is real, not estimated
  • Weekly summary shows spend, leads, bookings and closed revenue by service

What we need to launch

Roughly a week of setup on Google's side
  • Google Guaranteed verification with ROC #358547 and #361063 on file
  • Certificate of insurance and business license upload
  • Service area list confirmed by ZIP
  • Call answering and message routing assigned to a named person
  • Review requests turned on after every completed job

Why it wins here

Phoenix roofing search is crowded and expensive per click
  • Pay only for leads, so a slow month costs less
  • Google Guaranteed badge carries weight for insurance-driven roof decisions
  • Dual license in roofing and electrical is a real differentiator in the listing
  • Reviews compound: every finished job feeds the next lead
