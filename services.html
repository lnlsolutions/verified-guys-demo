Roof types

Roofing systems we install

Most common

Asphalt Shingle

The most widely used roofing material in the U.S. An excellent balance of affordability, durability, and curb appeal, fully compatible with all solar mounting systems.

Most common

Tile Roof

Concrete and clay tile is a hallmark of Southwest architecture. We specialize in careful tile removal and reinstallation around solar without breaking a single piece.

Most common

Foam Roof

Spray polyurethane foam creates a seamless, fully adhered insulation layer. Lightweight, energy-saving, and excellent for flat desert roofs.

Elastomeric Coating

A liquid-applied seamless coating that seals and protects flat or low-slope roofs against UV, heat, and water intrusion.

Wood-Shake

Hand-split shakes for a natural, rustic aesthetic with excellent insulation properties and real character.

Standing Seam Metal

Raised interlocking seams with concealed fasteners. Exceptionally durable and wind-resistant, ideal for solar performance.

TPO Roofs

Single-ply membrane that is highly reflective and UV-resistant. Heat-welded seams for a watertight finish on flat roofs.
